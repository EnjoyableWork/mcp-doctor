# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be welcoming, technically rigorous, and safe for people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable, and proportionate feedback;
- respecting privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them; and
- handling security-sensitive or personal information through the appropriate
  private route.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private correspondence,
or sustained disruption of project work.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject changes,
issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Enforcement should protect the
affected people, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and remain proportionate to
the behavior and its impact.

For immediate threats or platform-wide abuse, use GitHub's safety and abuse
reporting tools. For a project conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer
through a non-public contact method shown on their GitHub profile. Do not use a
public issue to disclose another person's private information. Security reports
use the separate private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
